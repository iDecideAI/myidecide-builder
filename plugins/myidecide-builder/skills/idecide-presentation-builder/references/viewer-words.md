> **Reference for the myiDecide Presentation Builder skill.** The words the
> build writes on a slide ITSELF, in every language the myiDecide AI ships —
> generated from its language bundles, never edited by hand. When a
> presentation is written in one of these languages, use the row VERBATIM
> for the cover pill and the navigation buttons: the composer recognises
> Move Ahead / Finish Up / Continue by these exact words (a button whose
> label starts with one is the featured one; on a menu it is an exit, not a
> tracked topic). Everything the build matches by text — Slide Names, keys,
> shortcodes — stays English whatever the row.
